# Atom Loading globals (selected)

These live under `globals/Atom Loading` in each shot HDF5:

- green_mot_frequency: frequency of the green_mot on handoff of atoms to the cavity, in relative MHz; typical operating frequency of 48.9, safe range 48-50.
- z_bias_field_loading: Bias field along the z-axis during lattice loading; typical operating frequency of -0.101, safe range -0.110 to -0.090.
